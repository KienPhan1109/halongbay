--- HA LONG BAY PRESENTATION SCRIPT (LONG VERSION) ---
Level: English A2
Tips: Speak slowly, smile, use hand gestures, and look at your friends and the audience!

---

1. KIÊN (Introduction - Hero Section)
[Smile and look at the audience]
"Hello everyone! Good morning. My name is Kiên. Today, our group is very happy to be here. We will take you on a journey to a very famous and beautiful place in Vietnam. It is Ha Long Bay! 

[Point to the screen]
As you can see on the screen behind me, it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is located in the north of Vietnam. Every year, millions of tourists from all over the world come to visit this majestic wonder. Why do they love it so much? What makes it so special? Well, my friend Tín will answer these questions and tell you more about its nature. Please welcome, Tín!"

---

2. TÍN (Nature Section)
[Point to the "Natural Beauty" slide/section]
"Thank you, Kiên. Hello everyone, I am Tín. Let's look at the natural beauty of Ha Long Bay. It is incredibly beautiful and very big. In fact, there are about two thousand limestone islands here! 

Look at this picture! The water is always emerald green and very calm. Some rock formations look like animals, like a dog or a chicken. There is also an old story about this bay. People say that dragons came down from the sky to make these islands. We also have amazing big caves inside the rocks. It is truly a natural picture. Next, Khải will show you some fun activities you can do here."

---

3. KHẢI (Activities Section)
[Smile and point to the three activity cards on screen]
"Thanks, Tín. Hi guys, I am Khải. So, what can you do when you visit Ha Long Bay? There are many, many fun things! 

First, you can take a relaxing boat cruise. You can sit on the boat, drink coffee, and take beautiful photos of the islands. You can even sleep on the boat at night! Second, if you like sports, you can go kayaking. It is very fun to paddle your small boat on the water. You can go very close to the limestone cliffs. Finally, you can walk inside the massive caves, like Surprise Cave. They have beautiful colorful lights inside. Now, Đăng will tell you about the delicious food."

---

4. ĐĂNG (Food & Culture Section)
[Point to the "Food & Culture" slide/section]
"Hello, I am Đăng. Welcome to the food and culture section! The food in Ha Long Bay is amazing. Because it is next to the sea, the seafood is always very fresh and delicious. 

You can eat great grilled squid, fresh fish, and big crabs. You must try the special squid sausage, it is very famous! Also, you can visit the floating villages. This is very interesting. The local fishermen build their small wooden houses on the water. They live, work, and even have small schools on the water. The people are very friendly and always smile. Finally, Hưng will end our presentation today."

---

5. HƯNG (Conclusion & Q&A Section)
[Smile and point to the "Thank You" slide/section]
"Thanks, Đăng. Hello everyone, my name is Hưng. To sum up, Ha Long Bay is a perfect place for your next holiday. It has everything you need: beautiful nature, exciting activities on the water, and great fresh food. 

The best time to visit is in the summer or autumn when the sky is clear and blue. We hope you can visit Ha Long Bay one day and see it with your own eyes! 

[Look around the audience, smile warmly]
Thank you very much for listening to our presentation. We hope you enjoyed our journey today. Now, we have some time left. Do you have any questions for us?"
